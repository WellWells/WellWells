# Hi, I'm Wells 👋

Radar systems engineer in Taiwan. I came up through RF — antennas, circuits, measurement — but these days I spend less time on the front end alone and more on the system around it: signal chain, firmware, integration, and making the whole thing behave in the field.

I studied electrical engineering at NTUST, where my research was a circularly polarized self-oscillating active integrated antenna for wireless charging, plus 24 GHz and 77 GHz antenna arrays. That's where the RF background comes from; the software half I picked up because the problems kept crossing over.

I write at [**wellstsai.com**](https://wellstsai.com/en/) — tutorials, tool reviews, trip notes, and the occasional rant. The cat in the logo is Xiao Mi; the blog started as a way to keep that memory somewhere.

> Facts speak for themselves.

## What I work on

- **Radar systems** — antenna array calibration, UWB positioning, and the signal chain from front end to output.
- **Hardware bring-up** — MCU and SoC selection, firmware, and the unglamorous part where the bench setup disagrees with the datasheet.
- **AI tooling** — Claude Code, Copilot, Antigravity, local MCP servers, and keeping track of what the pricing actually costs.
- **Networking and privacy** — Tailscale, Cloudflare, mDNS, and a home lab that got more complicated than it needed to be.
